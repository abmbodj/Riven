[04:25] Two, necessities versus luxuries. Necessities tend to be inelastic, luxuries tend to be elastic.
[04:37] Three, the share of your budget. Something that's a tiny share of your budget, like salt, tends to be inelastic, because you barely notice price changes.
[04:52] Four, time horizon. Demand gets more elastic over time. When gas prices spike, people can't change much this week, but over a few years they buy more efficient cars or move closer to work.
