# Target student persona (onboarding)

- **Motivation:** pass the next exam with less cramming anxiety; wants tools that feel fast and “smart,” not another bloated LMS.
- **Behavior:** studies in bursts (mobile), sometimes deep work (laptop); low tolerance for long forms or abstract feature lists.
- **Jobs-to-be-done:** turn lectures/readings into something they can rehearse (notes → cards → practice).
- **Friction to avoid:** empty dashboards, unclear “what now,” typing on mobile, mandatory uploads before any value.

Onboarding copy and CTAs should reinforce **immediate capture** (audio/notes) and **optional structure** (syllabus/class), not account setup.
