## Design Context

### Users
College and high school students studying for exams. They use Riven as their primary study tool — flashcards, notes, AI-generated tutoring sessions. Context: late-night study sessions, exam prep stress, need to feel calm and supported while learning. Mobile-first (Capacitor iOS app) but also desktop web.

### Brand Personality
Warm, scholarly, alive. Riven feels like a living study companion, not a clinical ed-tech tool. The mascot River (a grey cat in a green knit beanie) is central to the brand — friendly, witty, encouraging.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Base theme**: Dark botanical — deep teal backgrounds, warm parchment text, gold accents
- **Tutor/Lecture mode**: Warm vintage classroom — aged wood-framed chalkboard, slightly warm brown-green board surface, cream chalk text. Nostalgic lecture hall feel, like a great professor's office with worn wood and chalk dust
- **Anti-references**: Clinical white ed-tech (Coursera, Khan Academy web), gamified neon (Duolingo), sterile AI chat interfaces
- **References**: Vintage university lecture halls, hand-drawn mathematical proofs on chalkboards, the tactile warmth of old books and wooden desks

### Design Principles
1. **Lecture, not quiz** — The tutor should feel like sitting in a great professor's office while they explain something at the board, not like taking a standardized test
2. **Chalk is the medium** — Text appears as if written on the board. Animations should feel deliberate and paced, like someone writing in real-time
3. **Warmth over polish** — Slightly imperfect, organic textures over sterile gradients. Wood grain, chalk dust, soft edges
4. **River is the teacher** — River stands at the board, pointer in hand. The mascot drives the lecture, not a faceless AI
5. **Progressive depth** — Content reveals section by section like a real lecture. The student controls the pace by advancing through the lesson
